From a missed card to a word you use

Recognising a word and using it are two different skills. Recognition comes first and is quick to train. Using the word takes practice of its own. The flow trains them in that order, then keeps bringing each word back just before you would forget it.

The path

  1. 1

    B1 Recall collect

    Play the B1 flashcards. The words you miss pile up in the unknown list.

    Once a batch has piled up, keep the words worth learning and turn them into a new Vocabulary group. Easy or rarely useful words can stay behind.

  2. 2

    Vocabulary recognise

    Work the group with the list, flashcards, quiz and fill-in. The practice score is yours to play with. Reset a group and run it again as often as you like; the flow keeps its own record.

    Know a word on two different days and it moves to sentence practice by itself. Two right answers in the same session only count once, because remembering something you saw five minutes ago is short-term memory. A wrong answer starts the count over.

  3. 3

    Articles context

    An article puts a group's harder words into a real passage. Not every word needs one: the easy ones are already settled, so the article focuses on the words you still need to see in context. Read it once you know the meanings and before you write sentences.

  4. 4

    Collocations patterns

    Which words each word keeps company with. Go over them right before writing sentences so the patterns are fresh.

  5. 5

    Sentence practice use

    The words that passed step 2 sit in Vocabulary's Sentences tab. Copy the list, write your own sentences with each word and have them checked, by a teacher, a study partner or an AI chat.

    Once you have used a word in your own sentences, mark it Learned. It moves to Review.

  6. 6

    Review keep

    Each word gets two calendars: flashcards (Turkish to English) and sentence practice. The Review page lists what is due today. For sentences, copy the list, practise wherever you write and get feedback, then come back and mark the date.

Rules worth knowing

Shortcut for easy words

If a word is obvious, mark it Learned straight away. It skips sentence practice and enters Review at Low priority.

Falling back

Miss a Review card twice in a row and the word goes back to sentence practice, with its Learned mark removed. Using a forgotten word again fixes it better than seeing it on a card.

Priority

Words arrive in Review at Medium. Raise the hard and useful ones to High, drop the simple ones to Low. Priority sets the spacing, and High words come first on a busy day.

Undoing Learned

Remove the Learned mark in Vocabulary and the word leaves Review. It goes back to sentence practice.

Every success moves a word one step to the right; the last step repeats. A missed flashcard goes back to the first step and returns the next day. Changing priority recalculates the next date from the last time you reviewed the word.

Outside the flow

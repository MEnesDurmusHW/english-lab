    Choosing the word
    1. What caused it? Relief comes after a fear, excitement after good news.
    2. How strong is it, and how long? Irritated is a spark, furious is a fire, resentful is the smoke that stays.
    3. Who is listening? Delighted and underscore suit an email to a client; thrilled and snag suit a friend.
